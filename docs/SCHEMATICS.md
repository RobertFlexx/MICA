# Hardware drawings

The [component inventory](../schematics/current/component-ports.json) and
editable DOT/SVG/PDF interfaces for SPI, portable UART TX, the VHDL event counter
and watchdog are generated by `scripts/generate_component_schematics.py`.
The enabled SoC drawing includes the watchdog connection. These are RTL
engineering diagrams, not electrical PCB schematics.

The [drawing index](hardware/DATAPATH.md) lists the original 31 refreshed diagram paths:
the six 2.0 overviews, six elaborated transactional/SoC drawings and nineteen
existing top-level paths. Those drawings have DOT, SVG, PNG and PDF versions;
the four additional component interfaces above have DOT, SVG and PDF versions.

The [2.0 implementation guide](hardware/MICA2_IMPLEMENTATION.md) explains the
changed decode, stack operations and sleep/wake paths. Core and SoC inventories
come from Yosys elaboration with MICA2=1; a SHA-256 manifest detects stale sources
and rendered previews. Multicore and split-slice proposals are labeled as research.

These are architectural/connectivity drawings. Physical pin assignments and
the board vendor's electrical references are in
[board connections](hardware/BOARD_CONNECTIONS.md).
